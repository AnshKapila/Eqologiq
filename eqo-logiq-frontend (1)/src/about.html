Our Story

Eqo Logiq makes plastic-free steel bottles and daily essentials.

A note from our founder

Health and sustainability were always important to me, until I saw how badly the basics were failing both.

Being into health & sustainability from an early age, after reading about the damage to us and the environment.

I was shocked to see there's still no truly plastic-free option in something as basic and necessary as water bottle considering that its the one thing we consume the most and also see being littered the most.

And whenever shopping for sustainable its easier to see 'green branding and claims' but hard to see the real information about the material used.

So to bridge the gap and make an impact I started Eqo Logiq.

Homegrown and Bootstrapped.

Vaibhav

Founder, Eqo Logiq

How We Got Here

From the Beginning

2022 01

The decision.

After deciding to pursue sustainability as a career, the search began for plastic-free daily essentials. It was surprising to find that not a single steel bottle in the market was completely free of plastic. No matter which bottle you picked, there was plastic in the cap or the coating.

2023 02

The first bottle.

Introduced our bottle into a crowded market of water bottles. Reach was slow to begin with.

2024 03

Learning out loud.

We made mistakes. From the beginning we were not able to deliver a fully plastic-free experience: there was plastic left in places we had not resolved yet. We came back with a better bottle, more options, and a product that was finally completely plastic-free.

2025 04

Early traction.

Started seeing traction, trust, and repeat customers. Growing from feedback.

Now →

Improving and expanding.

Working on improving the experience and building on what is working.
