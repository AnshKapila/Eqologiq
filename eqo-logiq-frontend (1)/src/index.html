The Switch

What changes when you remove plastic from daily use.

With plastic

With plastic

  • BPA and phthalates from plastic leach directly into liquids especially under heat or repeated use.
  • Microplastics from plastic bottles have been found in human blood, lungs, and drinking water, causing life threatening diseases.
  • Plastic degrades within months, shedding harmful particles into every drink along the way.

With Eqo Logic

THE CAP RING

Silicone, not plastic.
