What it is, and why it matters.

This is the bottle we built because we were tired of options that compromised on either performance or principle. Plastic leaches into your water. Cheap steel rusts. And insulation that fails in Indian summer heat is insulation that isn't doing its job.

The 750ml Steel Water Bottle uses double-wall vacuum insulation tested at 42°C. A 360-degree leak-proof seal. A weight that feels intentional, not flimsy. A wide mouth opening for ice cubes and easy cleaning.

Available in 750ml and 1 Litre. Both sizes use the same food-grade stainless steel, the same insulation, and the same cap mechanism. Choose your size based on your day, not on compromise.

What it's made from.

18/8 Food-Grade Stainless Steel

The same grade used in surgical instruments and high-quality cookware. No BPA, no phthalates, no leaching — ever.

Silicone Seal Ring

Food-grade silicone gasket on the cap. Replaceable, dishwasher-safe, and leak-tested to 0.3 bar.

Powder-Coat Finish

Non-toxic, chip-resistant powder coat. Clean with a damp cloth. The bottle interior can be washed with warm soapy water.

Not Dishwasher Safe (Interior)

The exterior powder coat can go on the top rack. The bottle body should be hand-washed. This protects the vacuum seal.

The environmental case for this bottle.

The average Indian household goes through roughly 400 single-use plastic bottles per year. At 12 grams per bottle, that's nearly 5 kilograms of plastic waste annually — from water alone.

The Core Bottle is built to last a minimum of 10 years with normal use. If it replaces one plastic bottle per day, it prevents approximately 3,650 bottles from entering the waste stream over its lifetime. That's not a marketing claim — it's arithmetic.

The bottle ships in 100% recycled cardboard with no plastic wrap, no bubble wrap, and no single-use plastic of any kind in the packaging chain. The steel itself is 100% recyclable at end of life.
