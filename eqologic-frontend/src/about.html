Our Story

We believe better products naturally lead to better outcomes.

A note from the founder

I started Eqologic because I was exhausted by the options.

Every plastic bottle I'd owned had eventually made my water taste like the container it came in. The reusable ones weren't much better — cheap steel that rusted, lids that leaked, insulation that gave up by noon in a Mumbai summer.

I wasn't looking for an "eco brand." I was looking for a product that was simply better. What I found was that making something genuinely better — better materials, better engineering, better design — naturally eliminated the environmental problem too. The two things aren't in conflict. They're the same decision.

Eqologic exists because I believe sustainable shouldn't mean sacrifice. It should mean superior. Every product we make starts with the question: is this the best version of this thing that can exist? Not the most convenient. Not the cheapest. The best.

We build for people who have stopped settling. People who choose their daily-use products with intention. People who understand that what you use every day says something about how you think — and who don't want that statement to be "I didn't look for something better."

We're a small team. We're not backed by a conglomerate. We answer to the people who use our products, and we take that seriously.

Vaibhav

Founder, Eqologic

How We Got Here

The short version.

1

2021

The frustration point.

After going through four "eco" water bottles in two years — all leaking, rusting, or breaking — the idea for Eqologic was written on a notebook page in a Mumbai co-working space.

2

2022

First products, first customers.

The Core Bottle launched in three colours. We sold 200 units in the first month — mostly word of mouth. The bamboo brush and copper tongue cleaner followed six months later.

3

2023

10,000 households.

We passed 10,000 orders and launched corporate gifting partnerships with teams who were done with generic plastic swag. Our first NGO partnership for clean water access went live.

→

Now

Still small. Still deliberate.

We're not trying to be the biggest brand. We're trying to be the one you trust completely. Every product that ships under the Eqologic name is something we'd use ourselves without hesitation.
