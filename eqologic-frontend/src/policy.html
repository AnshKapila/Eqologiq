Policies & Terms

Last updated: January 2025

Free Shipping Threshold

Shipping is free on all orders above ₹999. For orders below ₹999, a flat shipping charge of ₹79 applies at checkout regardless of destination within India.

Delivery Timeframes

Standard delivery to metro cities (Mumbai, Delhi NCR, Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Chennai, Pune, Kolkata) takes 3–5 business days. All other pin codes in India take 5–7 business days. Express delivery is available in select metros at an additional charge and delivers within 1–2 business days.

Order Processing

Orders placed before 2:00 PM IST on business days are typically dispatched the same day. Orders placed after 2:00 PM or on weekends and public holidays are dispatched the next business day. You will receive a dispatch confirmation email with tracking details within 24 hours of your order shipping.

International Shipping

We currently ship within India only. International shipping is planned for 2025. If you would like to be notified when international shipping launches, please contact us at hello@eqologic.com.

Damaged or Lost Shipments

If your order arrives damaged, please email hello@eqologic.com with your order number and a photograph of the damage within 48 hours of delivery. We will arrange a replacement shipment at no cost. If tracking shows your package as delivered but you have not received it, please contact us within 72 hours and we will initiate a courier investigation.

Return Window

We offer a 15-day return window from the date of delivery. To be eligible for a return, products must be unused, in original condition, and in original packaging.

Non-Returnable Items

For hygiene reasons, oral care products (toothbrushes, tongue cleaners) are non-returnable once packaging has been opened. These products are eligible for exchange or refund only if they are defective or damaged upon arrival.

How to Initiate a Return

Email hello@eqologic.com with your order number and reason for return. We will respond within 24 hours with a prepaid return label. Refunds are processed to the original payment method within 5–7 business days of receiving the returned item.

Defective or Incorrect Items

If you receive a defective, damaged, or incorrect item, we will replace it at no charge. Contact us within 48 hours of delivery with your order number and a photograph. Replacement shipments are dispatched within 2 business days.

Bundle Returns

Bundle products can be returned as a complete set for a full refund, or individual items within the bundle can be returned for a partial refund based on the per-item bundle price. Oral care items within bundles are subject to the standard hygiene policy above.

Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly: name, email address, shipping address, phone number, and payment information (processed securely by Razorpay — we do not store card details). We also collect usage data when you visit our website, including pages viewed, referral sources, and device type.

How We Use Your Information

  • To process and fulfil your orders
  • To send order confirmations, shipping notifications, and customer service communications
  • To send marketing communications if you have opted in (you can unsubscribe at any time)
  • To improve our website and product offering
  • To comply with legal obligations

Data Sharing

We do not sell or rent your personal information to third parties. We share your information only with service providers necessary to fulfil your order (courier partners, payment processors) and only to the extent required to perform those services.

Cookies

We use cookies to maintain your session, remember cart contents, and understand site usage via analytics. You can disable cookies in your browser settings, though this may affect your shopping experience.

Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data at any time by contacting hello@eqologic.com. We will respond within 15 business days.

Contact
